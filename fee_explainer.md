# ChatGPT Free limits and the ChatGPT Go subscription — what to know

**Issue identified from reviews:** users on the Free plan hit limits on images and uploads, then see upgrade prompts, and are unsure what is free, what paying for ChatGPT Go changes, and how billing and cancellation work in the Android app.

- **Everyday text chats are unlimited on both Free and Go.** Image generation, file uploads and data analysis have their own separate limits.
- **ChatGPT Go raises those tool limits; it does not remove them.** Go adds higher limits for image generation, file uploads and advanced data analysis, plus a larger context window. Voice mode has the same limits as Free.
- **Limits can change.** On every plan, usage is subject to plan limits, abuse-prevention safeguards and temporary restrictions during periods of high demand.
- **Go renews automatically every month until you cancel.** Current prices are listed on the ChatGPT pricing page and may be adjusted over time. Refunds for incorrect charges go through OpenAI's refund request process.
- **If you subscribed in the Android app, Google Play bills you.** To cancel, open the Play Store, go to Menu > Subscriptions > ChatGPT > Cancel subscription. Uninstalling the app does not cancel it.
- **After you cancel, paid features stay active until the current period ends.** Google Play charges about 24 hours before a period ends, so cancel before then to avoid the next charge.
