# ChatGPT India: Weekly Product Pulse
**Week of 13 Oct 2026.** Source: 44,567 Google Play reviews (India), 4 Aug–12 Oct

**Top themes** (share of substantive 1–3★ reviews)
1. **Free-plan limits & upgrade pressure (22%).** Image caps of 1–3, attachment limits and hours-long cooldowns, then upgrade prompts.
2. **App bugs & update regressions (14%).** Endless sign-in loading, crashes, broken select-all/copy, missing dark mode.
3. **Answer quality (14%).** Confident wrong answers and ignored instructions; users cross-check with Gemini.

**What users say**
- "Why call it a free app if image generation is so limited?"
- "it is stuck on an endless loading for sign in."
- "har 10 mcq me se 4 galat bata ta haiii." *(gets 4 of 10 MCQs wrong)*

**Key observation**
Limit complaints are growing: their share of 2★ reviews rose from ~15% in early August to 24–29% since mid-September. Users object less to limits than to not understanding them. Limits feel random ("some users have 1 picture… some 3-5"), reset times are unclear, and some report chat locking even though text chat is unlimited. About 950 "image" complaints are really about limits.

**Actions for the product team**
1. Show remaining images/uploads and the reset time in-app; verify text chat stays available after a tool limit is hit.
2. Rewrite the upgrade screen to state exactly what Go raises and what stays limited; link the new support snippet on limits and billing.
3. Open P1 bugs for the sign-in loop and the select-all/copy regression; track both next week.
